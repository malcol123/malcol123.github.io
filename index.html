Martina Columbaro

I am a PhD student at the Italian Institute of Technology (IIT), where I am a member of the Humanoid Sensing and Perception (HSP) group, led by Lorenzo Natale.

Previously, I earned a double-degree MSc in Mechatronic Engineering and Automation and Robotics from Politecnico di Torino and Universidad Politécnica de Madrid. My research journey began at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), where I completed my Master's thesis under the supervision of Prof. Cristina Piazza and Dr. Patricia Capsi-Morales.

Research

My research explores how multimodal imitation learning can enable more autonomous and intuitive control of upper-limb prostheses. My main interests include:

  • Imitation learning for robotic grasping and manipulation;
  • Manipulation with upper-limb prostheses;
  • Egocentric and eye-in-hand vision for assistive robotics;
  • Multimodal learning for intelligent prosthetic control;
  • Tactile sensing and human-robot interaction.
